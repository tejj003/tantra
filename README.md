# Tantra

**Art & music by Tejj**

[Experience Tantra](https://tejj003.github.io/tantra/)

## Artist Statement

What happens when a painting is allowed to unfold in time?

Tantra is a three-part encounter between music, colour and geometry. Drawing on the visual language of Neo-Tantric abstraction, it treats the centre as a place of both stillness and change. Forms gather, open, turn and return. Sound is not simply an accompaniment: its pulse becomes a way for the image to breathe.

Each work approaches that relationship differently. The first moves through interlocking forms and expanding fields of colour. The second builds an architecture of stepped triangles, circles and mirrored planes. The third opens into a landscape of clouds, leaves and water, where geometry takes on a softer, living rhythm.

A white frame holds these movements together. Within it, intensity gives way to quiet, and each composition finds its way back to rest. The series invites sustained attention to the space between what is heard and what is seen, between a pattern that changes and a centre that remains.

These are contemporary artistic interpretations, not reproductions of sacred diagrams or claims of ritual meaning.

## Viewing

Choose a work and press Play. Fullscreen begins with a six-second silent artist statement before the music and artwork unfold. Headphones and a landscape display are recommended.

The timeline lets you revisit a passage. Motion can be turned off while the music continues, and reduced-motion preferences are respected. The works contain vivid patterns and movement.

Save frame creates a 1080p still. Download video renders the complete work with its opening statement and soundtrack in supported browsers; a desktop browser is recommended. Rendering happens on your device and can take several minutes. No microphone, camera or account is required.

## Run Locally

Requires Node.js 22 or later.

```sh
npm ci
npm run dev
```

For a production build, run `npm run build`. Browser checks run with `npx playwright install chromium webkit` followed by `npm test` after building.
